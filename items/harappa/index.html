How to play

Controls

Your mission is at the top of the screen: tap it to see all six and choose any one. Walk to the place and press Look closer. Show the way draws glowing dots to your mission. Yellow diamonds mark more things to discover. Press Time travel to switch between the ruins of today and the living city of about 2500 BCE.

The city is modelled on Mohenjo-daro, one of the main cities of the Harappan civilisation. The Great Bath and the large buildings of the upper town follow the excavation plans. The other buildings and all the houses are typical examples, not copies of real ones, and distances are shortened. Roofs, upper floors and the wooden hall are reconstructions: only the brick walls survive. The photos in the cards show real places and objects.

Mohenjo-daro · a Harappan city

Harappa Explorer

Walk through the ruins of a Harappan city, about 4,500 years old. Then travel back in time and see it alive.

  1. Six missions: walk there and find them, in any order.
  2. Tap Time travel to go back to about 2500 BCE.
  3. Yellow diamonds mark more to discover.
