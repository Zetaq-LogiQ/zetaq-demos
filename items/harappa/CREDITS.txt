Reconstruction: the city is modelled on Mohenjo-daro. Roofs, upper floors, the wooden hall, carts and boats are
reconstructions (only brick walls survive); distances are shortened.

Software: three.js r128 + GLTFLoader (MIT licence); Nunito typeface (SIL Open Font Licence).
