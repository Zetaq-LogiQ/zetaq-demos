Sizes, distances, gravity and today’s planet positions are real. The one cheat: your ship can fly faster than light, so trips take seconds instead of years.

Maps and elevation data: NASA, JPL-Caltech, USGS Astrogeology, NOAA, JHUAPL/SwRI, LPI, DLR, Space Science Institute, Solar System Scope (CC BY 4.0) and Wikimedia Commons. Stars: HYG Database (CC BY-SA 4.0). The full list of sources and licences is in CREDITS.md.

Kennedy Space Center · Launch pad 39A

Solar System Explorer

Your spaceship is ready. Fly to any planet or moon, land, and walk on it in your spacesuit.

  1. Launch, then pick a world. The ship flies you there.
  2. Land and step outside. Real gravity: jump high on the Moon!
  3. Scan the ground and collect samples for your logbook.
