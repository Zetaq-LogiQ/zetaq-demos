# Credits

Texture maps in `tex/` and the star catalogue in `js/stars.js` come from the sources below.
All maps are equirectangular (plate carrée), north up, east to the right, with longitude 0° at the horizontal centre.
"Processing" lists every change made to the downloaded file; unlisted files are byte-identical copies.

Abbreviations: **SSS** = Solar System Scope textures, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/ (CC BY 4.0, attribution "Solar System Scope").
**NASA-3D** = NASA 3D Resources, https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/tree/master/Images%20and%20Textures (NASA/JPL-Caltech maps; NASA media, not copyrighted, credit requested).
**PD** = public domain (US Government work).
